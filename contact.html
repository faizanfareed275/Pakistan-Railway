Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries

Visit the Book Ticket page, enter your journey details, select your train and class, choose seats, enter passenger details, and pay securely online. Your e-ticket will be sent to your email and phone instantly.

Cancellations made 24+ hours before departure receive a 90% refund. Cancellations 2–24 hours before receive 50%. No refund within 2 hours of departure. Refunds are processed within 72 hours to your original payment method.

Yes, both printed and digital (on your phone) e-tickets are accepted. You must present your ticket along with a valid CNIC/passport that matches the passenger name on the ticket. No ticket transfers are permitted.

Children under 5 travel free (no seat). Children aged 5–11 receive a 50% fare reduction. Senior citizens aged 65+ receive a 25% discount. Persons with disabilities qualify for a 50% discount with valid documentation.

Visit the Live Train Status page. Enter your train number or route to see real-time location, delay information, and expected arrival times. You can also opt in for SMS alerts at time of booking.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, HBL, MCB, and UBL debit/credit cards, JazzCash, Easypaisa, and internet banking from all major Pakistani banks. All online payments are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.
