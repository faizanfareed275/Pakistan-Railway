Check PNR Status

Enter your PNR or booking reference number to check the current status of your ticket.

Where to find your PNR?

Your PNR is on your e-ticket email/SMS, printed ticket, or booking confirmation screen. It starts with "PKR" followed by 10 digits.

Accessibility Services

Pakistan Railways is committed to providing accessible travel for all passengers.

Wheelchair Access

Ramps at all major stations. Dedicated wheelchair spaces on all express trains.

Platform Assistance

Request assistance at the booking counter or online. Staff will escort you to your seat.

Visual Impairment

Braille timetables available on request. Guide dogs permitted on all services.

Hearing Impairment

Visual departure boards at all stations. SMS alert service available.

To pre-arrange accessibility assistance, call our dedicated helpline: 117 (press 3) or email accessibility@pakrail.gov.pk at least 24 hours before travel.

Travel Tips

  • Arrive at the station at least 20 minutes before departure.
  • Carry a valid CNIC or passport — required for ticket verification.
  • Download your e-ticket before travelling in low-signal areas.
  • Confirm seat numbers from the coach display board on the platform.
  • Report lost items to the Station Master immediately.
