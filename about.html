About Pakistan Railways

Connecting Pakistan through safe, reliable, and modern rail services since 1861.

Our Story

Pakistan's Rail Network – Over 160 Years of Service

Pakistan Railways traces its origins to 1861 when the first passenger line opened in the Sindh region. Today, it is one of the largest railway networks in Asia, stretching over 8,000 kilometres and connecting more than 500 stations from Karachi to Peshawar, and from Lahore to Quetta.

As the national rail operator under the Ministry of Railways, Government of Pakistan, we carry over 3 million passengers and thousands of tonnes of freight every month — playing a vital role in the country's economy, trade, and social connectivity.

Freight & Cargo

Reliable Freight Solutions

Pakistan Railways provides cost-effective, reliable freight services for businesses and individuals. Our rail freight network is ideal for bulk commodities, agricultural produce, petroleum products, and manufactured goods.

Agricultural Goods

Grain, wheat, rice, and other agricultural produce transported efficiently.

Petroleum Products

Tanker wagons for safe transport of oil, gas, and petroleum products.

Container Services

ISO container transport connecting ports to inland destinations.

Industrial Goods

Steel, cement, fertiliser, and other industrial materials.

Get a Freight Quote

Why Choose Rail Freight?

Cost Effective: Rail is up to 3x cheaper per tonne-km compared to road transport for bulk goods.
Environmentally Friendly: Rail freight produces 75% less CO₂ per tonne-km than road freight.
High Capacity: A single freight train can carry the equivalent of 60 road trucks.
Reliable: Dedicated freight corridors with predictable schedules and tracking.
Safe: Lower accident rates compared to road transport for hazardous materials.

Modernisation & Projects

Investing in Pakistan's rail future

Infrastructure

ML-1 Upgradation Project

The 1,872 km Main Line 1 from Karachi to Peshawar is being upgraded for speeds up to 160 km/h, reducing travel times by up to 40% and increasing capacity significantly.

New Fleet

246 New Passenger Coaches

Pakistan Railways is procuring 246 new passenger coaches with modern amenities, improved safety features, and accessibility provisions to replace aging rolling stock.

Digital

Digital Transformation Programme

From automated ticketing kiosks to real-time GPS tracking and the new mobile app, Pakistan Railways is undergoing a comprehensive digital overhaul to enhance the passenger experience.
